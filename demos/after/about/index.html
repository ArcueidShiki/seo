About Vexlora

Vexlora builds small, free tools for self-directed traders. The position size calculator came from our own trading checklist: decide the risk first, then the size.

How the calculator works

Everything runs in your browser. We do not store the numbers you type. The formula and its limits are documented in our position sizing rules guide.

Editorial policy

Guides are written by engineers who trade, reviewed when the underlying rules change, and dated on every page. Nothing on this site is financial advice.
